                            Studying the true role of the PD-L1 protein.


We are conducting an experiment studying the PD-L1 protien , which cancer cells use as a protective shield against the immune systeme . the gool is to determine the true role of this protien , so we removed it from the cell to observe the outcome , so we have in this experiment :
 SC-control : the normal samples (sc-veh)
 KD-knockdown : the samples depleted of PD-L1 protein  


                          Digital tools used:


For this process, we used the SciPy and Pandas libraries with Python.

:

Interactive Visualization: We represented the identified genes on an interactive plot.
